About

Jeffrey Borders is an independent researcher, autodidact, and artist based in Wichita, Kansas, working across physics, philosophy, and fiction without institutional affiliation. His ongoing series, The Borders Chronicles, is a speculative science fiction saga released independently through Amazon KDP.

A note on the name — there's another published author who also writes as Jeff Borders: a Pacific Northwest–based writer of YA fantasy (The Diamond War) and Latter-day Saint nonfiction (The Antichrist Playbook). Different person, different books. If you're here for The Borders Chronicles, you're in the right place.
